Quick answers

How do I pay the Dart Charge?

Use the “Pay Dart Charge” button above, which opens the official GOV.UK page. Have your vehicle registration ready. Pay by midnight the day after you cross to avoid a penalty.

Is Toll Alert the official payment site?

No. It is an independent reminder app. Payment is always made on the operator’s official site.

Does the app work when it’s closed?

Yes. It detects crossings in the background and your location stays on your phone. See the privacy policy.
