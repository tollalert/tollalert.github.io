How it works

Set it up once. Then forget it.

  1. It detects the crossing

    Toll Alert notices when you’ve crossed the bridge, tunnel, or toll road — running in the background, even with the app closed.

  2. It reminds you until you pay

    A phone notification reminds you at various times of the day that you have not yet paid your toll fee. You can set your own reminder times in Settings.

  3. It takes you to the right site

    Tap to pay and a link takes you straight to the official payment site, so you can’t get scammed by going to the wrong payment website.

  4. You tap “Paid” once you have

    That turns the reminder off. Toll Alert doesn’t check with Dart Charge or TfL — tapping “Paid” just tells the app you’ve paid, it doesn’t prove it.

In the app

Clear, calm and impossible to miss

Dark, high-contrast screens made to be read at a glance, with a big gold button where it matters.

Private by design

Your journeys stay on your phone

On-device detection

Toll Alert only uses your location to spot the crossings in your list. It doesn’t track or store your journeys, and your location is never sent anywhere.

No accounts, no adverts

There is nothing to sign up for, nothing to sell and no tracking. Read the privacy policy for the detail.

Always the official site

The pay button only ever opens the operator’s own website, never a copycat site that adds its own fees.

Toll Alert is a reminder tool only. It can’t guarantee it will detect every crossing or that every alert will reach you, and you remain responsible for paying every toll, charge and penalty that applies to your journeys. Toll Alert is a subscription app billed through Apple; the price and any free trial are shown in the App Store before you subscribe, and you can cancel any time in your Apple ID settings.
