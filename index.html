Toll Alert

Drive through a UK toll crossing or the London ULEZ, and Toll Alert reminds you to pay before it turns into a fine.

How it works

  • Turn on monitoring once. Toll Alert watches for the crossings in the background.
  • When you pass through one, you get a notification with the pay-by deadline and a link to the operator's official payment site.
  • Tap "Mark as paid" and the reminder goes away.

Crossings covered

Dartford Crossing, London ULEZ, Blackwall and Silvertown Tunnels, Mersey Gateway and Silver Jubilee Bridges, Tyne Tunnel, Humber Bridge and Warburton Toll Bridge.

Private by design

Your location is processed on your phone and never sent anywhere. There are no accounts, no adverts and no tracking.
