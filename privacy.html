Toll Alert Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Toll Alert reminds you to pay UK road tolls and the London ULEZ charge after you drive through them. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your location and everything else the app knows about you stays on your phone. Subscription payments are handled entirely by Apple.

Location

With your permission, Toll Alert uses your device's location, including in the background, only to detect when you pass through a monitored toll crossing or enter the ULEZ. Location is processed on your device by the operating system's geofencing service. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and the app does not keep a history of where you have been.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally on your phone: the crossings it has detected (which crossing and when), whether you have marked each one as paid, your reminder times and settings, and a short diagnostics log of geofence events to help troubleshoot missed alerts. Deleting the app deletes all of this.

Subscriptions and purchases

Toll Alert is a subscription app. Payments are taken and processed by Apple through the App Store; we never see or store your card or payment details. To check that your subscription is active, and to restore it if you reinstall or change phone, the app asks Apple's App Store directly, using Apple's StoreKit framework on your device. We run no subscription server and use no third-party subscription or analytics service, so we receive no information about your purchases or about you. Family Sharing, if you use it, is run by Apple. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your Apple ID settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions).

Notifications

Alerts and payment reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We do not use push notification servers.

What we don't do

Toll Alert has no accounts and no advertising, and it does not track you. The App Store version collects no personal data, and we never sell it.

Payment links

When you tap "Open payment site", the toll operator's official website opens in your browser. Anything you enter there is governed by that operator's own privacy policy.

Children

Toll Alert is intended for drivers and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
